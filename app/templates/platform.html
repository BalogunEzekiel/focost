Financial integrity

Designed to keep financial meaning clear

FOCOST distinguishes cash-flow activity from information that changes an asset's value without representing a cash transaction.

Authoritative calculations

Financial dashboards, reports and AI explanations are designed around application-level calculations rather than independently recreated figures.

Cash-flow distinction

Investment funding and qualifying goal contributions remain part of cash-flow expense calculations, while non-cash investment valuation gains and losses do not become income or expense.

Traceable financial history

Financial activities are retained as structured records so users can review transactions and understand how reported figures were produced.

Security-conscious architecture

FOCOST incorporates authentication, authorization, CSRF protection, security headers, account-state controls and administrative access controls as part of the application architecture.

Responsible use
