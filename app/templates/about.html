The builder

From accounting to financial AI

Ezekiel Balogun began his professional journey in accounting and financial management and later expanded into economics, data analytics, software development and artificial intelligence. That combination shapes the way FOCOST is engineered: financial rules should be explicit, data should be useful, and technology should ultimately improve decisions.

His transition into data science, AI/ML and software engineering is driven by a practical interest in applying technology to finance, business, financial inclusion and productivity challenges in Nigeria and across Africa.

FOCOST grew from that journey into an AI-powered financial operating system designed to bring transaction management, budgeting, goals, investments, reporting and financial intelligence into one coherent experience.

Why FOCOST exists
