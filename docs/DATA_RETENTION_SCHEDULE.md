# FOCOST Data Retention Schedule — Baseline

Final periods require legal/accounting/privacy approval.

- Active account/profile: while account is active and for the approved post-closure period.
- Financial records: while required for the service, user export and applicable legal/dispute obligations.
- Authentication tokens: until used or expiry, then eligible for cleanup.
- Audit/security records: only for the approved security/accountability period.
- AI usage metadata: according to documented product/accounting requirements; avoid retaining raw prompts longer than necessary.
- Payment/subscription records: according to billing, accounting, dispute and legal obligations.
- Uploaded profile/document files: until deletion, replacement or documented retention requirement.
- Backups: follow a defined rotation schedule; expired backup copies must be securely disposed of.

A deletion request must distinguish data that can be deleted from data that must legally or operationally be retained.
