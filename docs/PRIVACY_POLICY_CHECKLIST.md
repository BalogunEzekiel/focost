# FOCOST Privacy Policy / Play Store Checklist

Before public release, publish a legally reviewed privacy policy at a stable HTTPS URL.

The policy should accurately describe:

- account and contact information collected;
- financial records entered by users;
- AI prompts and AI-generated responses;
- AI providers used to process requests;
- payment/subscription information and the payment provider(s);
- Paystack transaction references and subscription identifiers;
- notifications and email delivery;
- profile pictures and uploaded content;
- cookies/session technology;
- security controls;
- data retention;
- deletion/export rights;
- third-party processors;
- international data transfers where applicable;
- support contact;
- children's/privacy age restrictions if applicable;
- changes to the privacy policy.

Do not claim that FOCOST never processes data through third parties if AI, payment, hosting or email providers are used.

For Google Play, make the Data Safety declarations consistent with the actual Android application, SDKs, backend processing and privacy policy.
