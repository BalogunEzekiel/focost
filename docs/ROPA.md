# FOCOST Record of Processing Activities (Implementation Template)

| Processing | Data | Purpose | Legal basis to validate | Recipients/processors | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Account | name, email, phone | account/service | contract | hosting/database/email | per retention schedule |
| Financial management | income, expenses, goals, assets | requested financial tracking | contract | hosting/database | per retention schedule |
| AI coach | prompts + required financial context | requested AI analysis | contract/consent as legally applicable | configured AI provider | provider-specific documented period |
| Billing | payment reference, subscription identifiers | subscription/payment reconciliation | contract/legal obligation | payment provider | legal/billing retention |
| Security | IP, user agent, audit events | security/fraud/accountability | legitimate interest/legal obligation as applicable | hosting/security personnel | security retention schedule |
| Communications | notification/email preference | service notices | contract/consent as applicable | email provider | per preference/retention schedule |
| Profile media | avatar/uploaded content | profile features | contract/consent as applicable | configured storage | until deletion or purpose ends |

Complete and approve this document with the DPO/privacy adviser before launch. Do not treat this template as a final legal ROPA.
